# ezCater

> ezCater is a corporate and workplace catering marketplace connecting businesses with a nationwide network of local restaurants and caterers. The platform supports meal-program management, per-team and per-location spend controls, and centralized invoicing for workplace food solutions. ezCater does not publish a public developer API; integration is handled through its enterprise/partner sales channel.
